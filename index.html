The programme

Practical computer skills for the modern workplace.

This programme prepares learners for offices, businesses, schools and organisations where computers are used every day. It goes beyond switching on a computer: you will practise the tasks employers expect.

Operating Windows, writing and formatting professional documents, building spreadsheets, preparing presentations, communicating by email, researching online and using AI tools responsibly. Excel receives particular emphasis because so many workplace roles depend on accurate records, stock, attendance and reporting.

Learn by doing

Seventy per cent of class time is hands-on practice, not lectures. Every lesson ends with something you have made.

Workplace focused

Tasks mirror the documents, records and messages used in real offices — letters, registers, invoices and reports.

Built to last

Core computer skills come before AI, so you can work independently and judge when a tool is helping — or misleading.
