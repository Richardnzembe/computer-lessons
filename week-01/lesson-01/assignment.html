Device Recommendation Memo

Your first workplace task. Find computers around you, then recommend the right device for four staff roles and explain your choices in a one-page memo.

30 marks · Computer Fundamentals
Week 1 · Day 1 · Practical assignment

Simulated office task · Recommend a computer for each member of a small team

Your role: You have just been appointed as the office assistant at Dalswin Hardware & Supplies, a small business with a reception desk, two field sales officers, an accountant and a managing director. The manager has asked you to recommend one computer for four staff members. Your recommendations must fit the work each person does — and the budget must be sensible.

1Computer hunt 8 marks

Look around your home, school, workplace or neighbourhood. Find ten computers. Remember that computers are not always desktops: include at least one phone, tablet or embedded computer.

For each device, record:

  • where you found it;
  • what type of computer it is;
  • the job it does (one sentence).

Present your findings as a table. A sample row is shown below.

Accessory examples: printer, external monitor, scanner, external hard drive, laptop bag, surge protector, headset.

3Write a one-page memo 6 marks

Type a one-page memo to the manager recommending the devices. Use a computer if possible; a neat handwritten memo is acceptable if you have no access. Your memo must include:

  • a heading with To, From, Date and Subject;
  • a short opening sentence explaining the purpose;
  • one short paragraph per role with your recommendation and reasons;
  • a closing sentence offering to discuss the options;
  • your name at the end.

To: The Manager, Dalswin Hardware & Supplies

From: [Your name], Office Assistant

Date: [Today’s date]

Subject: Recommended computer devices for four staff members

Start your memo here…

Save the file as W1D1_Device_Memo_YourName. Keep a copy in your Work Documents folder — you will reuse this folder in the Week 1 assessment.

4Presentation and submission 4 marks

  • Check spelling, spacing and that the memo fits on one page.
  • Make sure your name and the date are correct.
  • Submit through the student portal or hand the printed copy to your instructor on time.
