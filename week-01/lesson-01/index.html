Introduction to Computers

What a computer is, what it does with information, where computers are used at work — and how to tell a desktop from a laptop, a tablet, a server and the tiny computers hidden inside everyday machines.

Learning objectives

By the end of this lesson, you can…

  • Explain what a computer is and why it is more than a screen and keyboard.
  • Describe the four jobs every computer performs: input, processing, output and storage.
  • Give examples of computers at work in offices, shops, schools, hospitals and banks.
  • Identify the main types of computers and their strengths and limitations.
  • Choose an appropriate device for a given workplace task and give reasons.

A computer is not magic. It follows instructions — your job is to learn which instructions to give it, and when to check the result.
