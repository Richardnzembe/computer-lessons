Hardware & Software Specification Sheet

Your second workplace task. Audit a real computer, explain what its parts do, then recommend clear specifications for three staff roles — hardware and software together.

30 marks · Computer Fundamentals
Week 1 · Day 2 · Practical assignment

Dalswin Hardware & Supplies

Simulated office task · Prepare specifications for three new workstations

30marks
SetWeek 1 · Day 2
DueEnd of Day 2
FormatWord document or paper

Your role: You are the office assistant at Dalswin Hardware & Supplies. The manager is replacing three old computers and has asked for a one-page specification sheet. Each computer must fit the work of the person who will use it — and the total budget must be sensible. You will inspect one computer you can access, explain its main parts, then recommend hardware and software for each role.

1System audit 8 marks

Choose one computer you can use — at home, at school or in a workplace. Inspect it and record:

  • eight hardware items (for example the monitor, keyboard, processor, RAM, storage, printer);
  • four software items (for example the operating system and three applications);
  • for each item, whether it is hardware or software, and the job it does in one sentence.

Present the findings as a table. A sample row is shown below. You do not need the exact model number — describe what you can see in the settings or on the label.

ItemHardware or softwareJob it does
KeyboardHardwareTypes text, numbers and commands into the computer

Tip: on Windows, open Settings → System → About to find the processor, RAM and Windows edition. Use This PC to see storage.

2Explain six core components 8 marks

Complete the table for the six components below. For each one, write what it does and what happens when it is too small or too weak.

Useful phrases: “8 GB RAM is enough for…”, “A dedicated graphics card is needed because…”, “An SSD will make…”. Always connect the choice to the task.

4Present and submit 4 marks

Put everything into a tidy one-page specification sheet. Type it if possible; a neat handwritten sheet is acceptable if you have no computer access. Your sheet must include:

  • a heading with the business name, your name and the date;
  • the audit table from Task 1;
  • the component table from Task 2;
  • the three recommendations from Task 3;
  • a closing line recommending one computer to buy first and why.

Save the file as W1D2_Spec_Sheet_YourName. Keep a copy in your Work Documents folder — you will reuse this folder in the Week 1 assessment.
