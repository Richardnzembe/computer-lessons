Remember: lock keeps your programs open, sign out closes your session, sleep saves power, and shut down switches the computer off safely. Never cut the power to switch off. Each item is worth one mark.

Choose the control that does each job. Write one answer on each line.

Word bank: Minimise · Maximise · Restore down · Close · Snap left / right · Alt + Tab · Task View · Show desktop

1Make the active window fill the whole screen.
2Hide the window in the taskbar without closing it.
3Return a maximised window to its previous size.
4End a program and remove its window from the screen.
5Place two windows side by side on the screen.
