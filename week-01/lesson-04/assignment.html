Keyboard & shortcuts quick guide

Create a clear one-page guide to help a new colleague use the mouse and keyboard confidently at work.

Task

Prepare a one-page reference sheet (handwritten neatly or typed) that explains:

  1. Five mouse actions and when to use each (5 marks).
  2. Six useful keyboard keys and what each does (6 marks).
  3. Five shortcuts, their actions and one suitable workplace use (10 marks).
  4. A short typing and posture checklist with three practical tips (6 marks).
  5. Clear headings, readable layout and accurate language (3 marks).

Submission

Submit a PDF or clear photograph through Google Classroom. Name the file Lesson4_QuickGuide_YourName. Include your name and class. Do not include passwords or private information.

Success checklist

  • Actions are accurate and explained in beginner-friendly language.
  • Shortcuts are written clearly (for example, Ctrl + S).
  • The page is easy to scan and free of avoidable spelling errors.
  • Examples relate to safe everyday computer use.
