Mouse, keyboard & shortcut quick guide

Show that you can use the mouse and keyboard accurately, then turn what you know into a clear, one-page guide for a new office colleague.

30 marks · Computer Fundamentals
Week 1 · Day 4 · Practical assignment

Dalswin Business Centre

Prepare a quick reference guide for a new member of the office team.

30marks
SetWeek 1 · Day 4
DueBefore Lesson 5
FormatOne-page PDF or document
SubmitGoogle Classroom
Your role: You are helping prepare a new colleague for a first day at Dalswin Business Centre. They have used a computer a little but want a simple reference they can keep beside their workstation. Create a practical guide that makes common mouse, keyboard and shortcut tasks clear.

1Demonstrate mouse control 5 marks

On a computer, demonstrate the actions below. Record the action you used or describe what you would do if a device is not available.

ActionWhat you did or would doCheck
Select a file without opening it
Open a desktop shortcut
Show options for a file
Move a window
Scroll through a page

2Explain keyboard keys 6 marks

Choose six useful keys. Explain the action each performs in language a new colleague will understand. Include at least one editing key and one navigation key.

KeyWhat it doesWhen it helps
Example: BackspaceRemoves the character before the cursorCorrect a typing error

3Build a shortcut reference 10 marks

Choose five shortcuts from the reading. For each, state the shortcut, its action and one safe workplace use.

ShortcutActionWorkplace use
Example: Ctrl + SSave the active fileSave a letter while working

4Add a typing and comfort checklist 6 marks

Include three practical tips that help staff type accurately and work comfortably. Include posture, accuracy and a proofreading reminder.

5Polish and submit your guide 3 marks

Arrange the information under clear headings on one readable page. Check spelling and ensure shortcut symbols are clear. Save as Lesson4_QuickGuide_YourName and upload a PDF or clear photograph to Google Classroom. Do not include passwords or private information.

Before you submit

Review your work, then tick each item as you confirm it.
