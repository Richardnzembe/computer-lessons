Mouse, keyboard, typing & shortcuts

Use the mouse and keyboard with confidence. Practise accurate typing, essential keys and shortcuts that make everyday workplace tasks easier.

Learning objectives

By the end of this lesson, you can...

  • ✓Use click, double-click, right-click, drag and scroll for the right task.
  • ✓Identify useful keys and edit text accurately.
  • ✓Type and proofread a workplace message with a comfortable posture.
  • ✓Use common shortcuts and explain their effect.

Good computer work is calm and accurate. Use shortcuts to save time, then check the result before you move on.
