Week 1 · Day 4 · Core skills

Mouse, keyboard, typing & shortcuts

Build accurate, comfortable control of the computer. Practise mouse actions, keyboard essentials, workplace typing and time-saving shortcuts.

2.5 hoursBeginnerPractice + quiz10-question quiz · 30-mark assignment

Learning objectives

  • Use click, double-click, right-click, drag and scroll appropriately.
  • Identify key keyboard groups and use editing keys accurately.
  • Enter and proofread a short workplace message with good posture.
  • Apply common Windows shortcuts and explain their effect.
