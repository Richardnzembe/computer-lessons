Move and navigate

Drag, drop and scroll

  • Drag: hold the button, move the pointer, then release.
  • Drop: check the destination before letting go.
  • Scroll: rotate the wheel or use a touchpad gesture.
Practise first with a window or harmless item. Moving a file can change its location.

Edit text

Enter, correct and format

Use Shift for one capital or the upper symbol printed on a key. Use Caps Lock for a run of uppercase letters.

Correct errors with Backspace or Delete. Place the cursor carefully before editing.

Type names, dates and amounts accurately. Check spelling and punctuation before sharing.

Typing well

Comfort and accuracy first

PositionFeet supported; shoulders relaxed; wrists straight.

Practice sequence

Build confidence in small steps

  1. Practise click, double-click, right-click and scroll.
  2. Locate and use the essential keys in a short typing task.
  3. Choose the correct shortcut for each workplace situation.
Use the practice lab and worksheet. Ask for help when an action behaves differently on your device.
