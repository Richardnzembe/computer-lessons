Guided computer workflow · 45 minutes

Complete every milestone

Use Documents or the safe practice location assigned by your instructor. Work with your own practice file only.

1 · Build (10 min)

Create Work Documents. Inside it, create Reports, Letters and Presentations. Show the folder tree to your partner or instructor.

2 · Draft and save (15 min)

Create a one-page office opening checklist with a title, date, purpose and at least five clear steps. Save it in the most relevant subfolder using File > Save As.

3 · Rename and verify (8 min)

Rename the practice document using a clear topic and date. Confirm the extension remains and reopen the file.

4 · Retrieve and copy (7 min)

Close the document, use File Explorer to find it again, and make a copy in a practice destination. Confirm the original remains.

5 · Safe delete decision (5 min)

Create a blank temporary file, confirm it is the practice item, delete it, and inspect the Recycle Bin. Restore it if the instructor requests. Do not delete real files.

No computer available? Complete the paper folder tree, write the document draft and record each Windows step. Arrange a brief device check with your instructor when equipment is available.
